*Field Research Assistant — MSU-IIT Thesis Study*

- Structured multi-variable environmental and survey data compliant with ISO 7730 and ASHRAE 55 thermal comfort standards.

*Lead Robotics Workshop Mentor*

- Led the Finch Robot station (micro:bit), guiding students through block-based logic (Snap!, BirdBlox) and sensor-driven obstacle negotiation.
- Assisted curriculum delivery spanning Arduino firmware integration, basic computer vision/object detection, and 3D fabrication prototyping.

- Strategizing to spearhead campus initiatives, collaborating with existing technology clubs to make quantum concepts digestible for non-specialists.
